Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

SubPulse ("we", "our", or "us") is designed with privacy first. This Privacy Policy explains how SubPulse handles your information.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

All subscription entries, notes, reminder preferences, and currency settings are stored strictly locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData and UserDefaults frameworks. We do not operate a backend server that stores your subscription data.

2. In-App Purchases

Optional SubPulse Pro subscriptions are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit / App Store. We never see or store your payment card details or Apple Account credentials. Please review Apple's Privacy Policy for information about how Apple handles billing data.

3. Local Notifications

If you grant notification permission, renewal reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. No push notification tokens or reminder contents are sent to external servers.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

SubPulse does not include third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or behavioral analytics.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete individual subscriptions or erase all local data at any time inside the app via Settings → Delete All Local Data, or by uninstalling the app.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email kiinkrft@gmail.com.
